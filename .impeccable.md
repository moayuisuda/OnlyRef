## Design Context

### Users
OnlyRef is for artists and visual creators who collect, compare, and organize image references while working. They need a local-first tool that stays out of the way, feels dependable during long sessions, and helps them move quickly between collecting, searching, and reviewing references.

### Brand Personality
Calm, refined, professional.

The product should feel focused and trustworthy rather than playful or flashy. It should support concentration, make image content the priority, and communicate that the library is well organized and under control.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark, editorial, studio-like.

Use restrained contrast, softly tinted neutrals, and a measured teal accent already present in the product. Favor layered surfaces, precise spacing, and quiet motion. The interface should feel composed and tool-like, not futuristic, neon, toy-like, or overly decorative.

### Design Principles
1. Image-first hierarchy: interface chrome should support the artwork, not compete with it.
2. Quiet confidence: visual emphasis comes from spacing, contrast, and material depth rather than saturated effects.
3. Structured clarity: controls should read as a coherent system with consistent panel, chip, and feedback patterns.
4. Studio-grade focus: interactions should feel responsive and polished, but never noisy or gimmicky.
5. Local-tool trust: the product should look dependable, precise, and production-ready for daily use.
